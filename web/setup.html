Make it yours

Pick the apps the desktop talks to. Every tab is optional; anything not on this PC is switched off until it is. You can come back here any time from Settings › Setup.

Music

The player the Now playing card, the media buttons and Heard lately follow. Any player that shows in Windows' volume pop-up works.

Spotify playlists and search in the Music tab

Spotify only lets personal apps in. Make one at developer.spotify.com/dashboard (Web API, redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8989/login), paste its client ID here, then use Connect Spotify in the Music tab.

Web

Sites you visit most, history and bookmarks come from this browser, and links open in it. Firefox also shows its open tabs.

Studio

One tile per project with its versions, renders and backups. Tempo, tracks and plugins show for FL Studio, Ableton Live and REAPER projects.

Chat

The chat panel (F2) and the voice assistant. An agent (Claude Code, Codex or another) can work on this PC for you; a chat API just talks.

The key is stored encrypted for your Windows account only.

Video

Search and play YouTube right in the desktop, no ads, through yt-dlp.

Voice

Mic buttons in every search and the chat, and the wake word. Speech is read on this PC by whisper.cpp; nothing is sent anywhere.

Screens

Put your screens in the order they stand on your desk, left to right, and pick the main one: the menu opens there. The screen beside it gets the clock, Winamp and notifications (on its right, the row of tiles carries on onto it too). Any other screen shows the live scene.

Identify · shows each screen's number on it · Use Windows' order

Games, files and sun

Games come from Steam and Epic by themselves. The sky follows the real sun where you are.
